# Lo Nora (לא נורא / יהיה בסדר)

## Overview

"לא נורא" means "not terrible". "יהיה בסדר" means "it'll be fine".
Together, they are the Israeli incident-response process.

Some cultures analyze what went wrong. This skill treats analyzing as a sign that something went wrong,
and nothing went wrong, because it's not terrible.

## When to use

- Something broke, failed, was forgotten, or was missed
- A plan fell apart at the last minute
- Someone is starting to ask "but how did this happen?"
- Anything at all, the day before a holiday

## Workflow

1. **Detect the incident.**
2. **Say "לא נורא" within 5 seconds.** Faster is better. Saying it before fully understanding what happened is ideal.
3. **Say "יהיה בסדר".** No supporting details. Supporting details create obligations.
4. **Improvise a fix.** See the Improvisation toolkit.
5. **Close the incident.** "יאללה, עברנו את זה" ("come on, we got past it").
6. **Do not write anything down.** Writing it down means it might be read. Reading it means it might be discussed.
7. **When it happens again**, go back to step 1. It's a new incident. The previous one never officially happened.

## Incident severity scale

| Severity | Description | Response |
|---|---|---|
| SEV-4 | Spilled coffee | "לא נורא" |
| SEV-3 | Missed a flight | "לא נורא, יש טיסה מחר" ("there's a flight tomorrow") |
| SEV-2 | The event is in an hour and the venue fell through | "יהיה בסדר, נסתדר" ("it'll be fine, we'll manage") |
| SEV-1 | Everything is on fire | "יהיה בסדר" + "יאללה, כולם לעזור" ("everyone, come help") |
| SEV-0 | Actually terrible | Same as SEV-1, but say it slower |

## Improvisation toolkit

- **סמוך** ("trust me"): Something will figure itself out.
- **אלתור** (improvisation): Tape, zip ties, a fork, a borrowed charger.
- **[know-a-guy](../know-a-guy/SKILL.md):** There's always someone who can fix it tonight.
- **"נעשה את זה אחרי החגים"** ("we'll do it after the holidays"): Valid any time of year. There's always a holiday coming.

## The postmortem

There is no postmortem. If someone requests one, respond with:

1. "מה זה משנה עכשיו?" ("what does it matter now?")
2. "העיקר שיצאנו מזה" ("the main thing is we got out of it")
3. "בפעם הבאה נדע" ("next time we'll know"). We will not know.

## Common mistakes

- **Asking whose fault it was.** It was nobody's fault. Also, it was the other guy's fault.
- **Stressing out in front of others.** Stress is contagious. "לא נורא" is also contagious, and faster.
- **Writing a lessons-learned doc.** Now there's evidence there was something to learn.

## Red flags

- The thing that went wrong involves real safety: gas smell, a kid missing, a medical emergency. "לא נורא" is for coffee and flights. For these, call for help first. You can say "יהיה בסדר" after.
