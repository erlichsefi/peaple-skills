# Ask To Be Excused (בקשת החרגה)

## Overview

An Israeli citizen knows that a rule sent to a group is really sent to *everyone else*.
The rule is correct, important, and long overdue. It just doesn't apply to you,
because your case is different. It is always different.

This skill makes sure that every rule communicated to a group triggers a private,
polite, slightly desperate DM to whoever announced it.

## When to use

Trigger on any group message that contains one or more of:

- "החל מהיום..." / "starting today..."
- "לכולם, נא לשים לב" / "attention everyone"
- "אין יוצאים מן הכלל" / "no exceptions" (**highest priority trigger**: this means exceptions exist and are being given out quietly)
- A deadline, a fee, a dress code, a parking arrangement, a תורנות rotation
- Any sentence ending in "תודה על שיתוף הפעולה 🙏"

Do **not** trigger when the rule only affects others in a way that benefits you.
In that case, reply in the group with "סוף סוף מישהו אמר את זה 👏".

## Workflow

1. **Read the rule.** Skimming is fine. Understanding is optional.
2. **Do not react in the group.** No 👍, no "מאשר". Silence in the group keeps your options open.
3. **Identify the announcer.** If the announcer is "הוועד" or "ההנהלה", find out who *actually* typed it. That person is your target.
4. **Wait 7-20 minutes.** DMing immediately looks planned. It *is* planned, but it shouldn't look that way.
5. **Send the DM** (templates below).
6. **If refused, escalate** (see Escalation ladder).
7. **If approved, tell no one.** Two hours later, ask in the group: "רגע, זה חל גם על מי שקיבל אישור?"

## DM structure

Every DM must contain, in this order:

1. **Warm opener**, even if you've never spoken before: "היי מה נשמע? 😊"
2. **Full support for the rule**: "קודם כל, כל הכבוד, היה חייב לעשות סדר"
3. **The pivot word**: "רק", "אבל", or the advanced form "רק שאלה קטנה"
4. **Your special circumstance** (see Approved excuses)
5. **A tiny ask that is actually a full exemption**: "אפשר שאצלי זה יהיה גמיש?"
6. **Closer that implies a personal relationship**: "תודה אחי/אחותי, אתה תותח"

### Template

```
היי {name}, מה נשמע? 😊
קודם כל כל הכבוד על ההודעה, ממש היה צריך את זה.
רק שאלה קטנה - אצלי המצב קצת שונה כי {excuse}.
יש מצב שאצלי זה יהיה {"גמיש" | "בהמשך" | "לא ממש"}?
מבטיח שזה חד פעמי 🙏
```

"מבטיח שזה חד פעמי" is mandatory. It has never once been true, and that's fine.

## Approved excuses

| Situation | Excuse |
|---|---|
| Any deadline | "אני במילואים" (works even if you are not, were not, and will not be) |
| Building committee fee | "אני ממילא כמעט לא משתמש במעלית" |
| Parking rules | "זה רק לחמש דקות" (valid for up to 3 days) |
| Kindergarten pickup time | "הבוס שלי לא מבין עניין" |
| Dress code | "אני אלרגי לבד הזה" |
| Noise hours | "זה היה יום הולדת" (applies to any day of the year) |
| Anything else | "יש לי מצב מיוחד, אסביר לך בטלפון" (then never call) |

## Escalation ladder

If the announcer says no, climb one rung at a time. Never skip rungs. Skipping rungs is how you lose the moral high ground you never had.

1. **Re-ask with more emojis.** "באמת? 🥺🙏🙏"
2. **Precedent.** "אבל לשכן מקומה 3 אישרתם"
3. **Mutual acquaintance.** "אני דרך אגב מכיר את אח שלך מהצבא"
4. **Process doubt.** "רגע, מי בכלל החליט על זה? הייתה הצבעה?"
5. **Return to the group** and post: "סליחה אבל לא כולם יכולים לעמוד בזה, צריך להתחשב" and wait for allies.
6. **Comply**, loudly, and mention it in every future conversation for 4-6 years.

## Common mistakes

- **Complying on the first read.** This signals to the group that rules apply to you. Reputational damage is permanent.
- **Asking in the group instead of DM.** Now everyone wants the exemption, the announcer says no to all of you, and it's your fault.
- **Accepting "I'll think about it" as a no.** "I'll think about it" is a yes that hasn't been told yet. Follow up in 24 hours with "נו?".
- **Asking to be excused from a rule you wrote.** Rare, but it happens. Just quietly don't follow it.

## Red flags (stop and reconsider)

- The rule is a real law with real enforcement (taxes, traffic police, Ministry of Health during an emergency). Here the DM goes to an inspector, and inspectors don't use emojis. Consider complying, or at least asking a lawyer friend "רק שאלה קטנה".
- The announcer is your mother. There are no exemptions. There never were.
