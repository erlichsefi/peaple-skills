# Haggle Everything (מה המחיר האחרון?)

## Overview

A price is not a fact. A price is the seller's first draft.
Paying the first price isn't polite, it's a failure to participate in the economy.

The goal isn't always to save money. The goal is to never be the פראייר (sucker).

## When to use

- Any purchase, at any price point, in any setting
- Especially fixed-price stores. The tag says the price is fixed. Not written that it's not negotiable (see [not-written-not-forbidden](../not-written-not-forbidden/SKILL.md)).
- Service quotes: plumbers, movers, contractors, wedding venues
- Renewals: phone plans, insurance, gym. Threatening to leave is a love language.

## Workflow

1. **Show mild interest, then disappointment.** Pick up the item, look at the price, put it down slowly. Sigh.
2. **Ask the magic question:** "מה המחיר האחרון?" ("what's the final price?")
3. **Counter at roughly half.** It doesn't matter what it's worth. Half is a tradition.
4. **Mention a competitor.** "בשוק הכרמל ראיתי בחצי" ("I saw it for half at Shuk HaCarmel"). You did not.
5. **Start walking away.** Slowly. You need to be callable-back.
6. **Settle.** Ask for something free on top: "תוסיף לי..." ("throw in...")
7. **Pay in cash, and ask for a discount for paying in cash.**

## Phrases by price level

| Situation | Phrase |
|---|---|
| Opening | "כמה זה בשבילי?" ("how much is it for me?") |
| Middle | "תעשה לי מחיר" ("make me a price") |
| Pressure | "אני לקוח קבוע" ("I'm a regular"), on your first visit |
| Closing | "יאללה, עגל" ("come on, round it down") |
| Post-deal | "ואם אני לוקח שניים?" ("and if I take two?") |

## Fixed-price environments

- **Supermarket:** Can't haggle at the register. Find a dented can and haggle about that.
- **Chain store:** Ask for the manager. Managers have discretion. Discretion is negotiable.
- **Government fee:** Ask if there's a reduced rate for "מקרים מיוחדים" (special cases). You are a special case. See [ask-to-be-excused](../ask-to-be-excused/SKILL.md).
- **Online:** Open the chat window and type "יש קופון?" ("got a coupon?")

## Common mistakes

- **Saying how much you love it.** The price just went up 20%.
- **Haggling over 5 shekels for 40 minutes, then tipping 50.** Actually, this is fine. It's about the principle.
- **Accepting the first "no".** The first "no" is the seller's opening offer on the "no".

## Red flags

- The seller is an old lady selling something she made herself. Pay full price. Pay a little more. Some things are worth not winning.
