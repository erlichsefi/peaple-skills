# Ma Pitom (מה פתאום)

## Overview

"מה פתאום" roughly means "no way", "absolutely not", "where did that come from?".
It's the default response to any new idea that wasn't yours.

But rejecting the idea is only phase one. The real skill is phase two:
adopting the idea later, as if it came to you in a dream.

## When to use

- Someone suggests a change to how you do something
- Your partner suggests a new route, a new restaurant, or moving the couch
- A coworker proposes a better process
- Your mother suggests anything at all

## Workflow

### Phase 1: Rejection (immediately)

1. **Hear the suggestion.** Don't evaluate it. Evaluating looks like considering.
2. **Say "מה פתאום".** Facial expression: half smile, half "you don't understand".
3. **Add one reason**, any reason. "זה לא יעבוד אצלנו" ("that won't work for us") covers 90% of cases.
4. **Change the subject.**

### Phase 2: Incubation (3-10 days)

5. **Think about it in private.** In the shower, in traffic. Realize it's actually a good idea.
6. **Do not tell the person who suggested it.** Under any circumstances.

### Phase 3: Adoption

7. **Implement the idea.**
8. **Announce it as your own:** "חשבתי על משהו..." ("I was thinking about something...")
9. **If the original person says "but that's what I said":** answer "לא, אתה אמרת משהו אחר" ("no, you said something different"). Then explain how your version is completely different (it isn't).

## Escalation of "מה פתאום"

| Level | Phrase | When |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "מה פתאום" | Default |
| 2 | "מה פתאום, באמת" | They repeated the suggestion |
| 3 | "השתגעת?" ("are you crazy?") | They brought data |
| 4 | "בחיים לא" ("never in my life") | They're right, and everyone can see it |
| 5 | (silence, then do it the next day) | Fast-tracked Phase 3 |

## Common mistakes

- **Saying "good idea" in the moment.** Now it's on record that the idea is theirs. Unrecoverable.
- **Adopting too fast.** Under 3 days, the connection is obvious. Wait at least until the weekend.
- **Adopting it in front of the person who suggested it.** Wait for them to be out of the room, or out of the country.

## Red flags

- The suggestion was "maybe go see a doctor about that". Skip Phases 1 and 2. Go straight to Phase 3, and you can still take the credit.
