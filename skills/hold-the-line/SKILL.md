# Hold The Line (שומר מקום)

## Overview

A queue is not a line. It's a negotiation with many participants and no chairperson.
Your position in it is not where you stand. It's where you can successfully claim to stand.

## When to use

- There's a queue and you are not at the front of it
- You were in this queue earlier today, or this week, or at a similar branch
- You see someone you know anywhere in the queue

## Workflow

1. **Never ask "מי אחרון?"** ("who's last?"). Asking admits there is a last, and that it might be you.
2. **Scan for an anchor.** Anyone you know, anyone you made eye contact with earlier, or a bag on a chair that could be yours.
3. **Claim a position** using one of the techniques below.
4. **Hold eye contact with the clerk, not the queue.** The queue is not your audience.
5. **Once served, add one more thing.** "ורק עוד משהו קטן..." You're already at the front. Use it.

## Techniques

| Technique | Line | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Just a question** | "אני רק שאלה" | Turns into a 25-minute transaction. That's expected. |
| **Was here before** | "הייתי פה לפני, יצאתי רגע" | "רגע" can be up to 40 minutes. |
| **Saved spot** | "הוא שומר לי" | Point vaguely. Any man in a cap will do. |
| **The number** | Wave a number from the machine | Don't let anyone read it. It's from yesterday. |
| **The emergency** | "יש לי חניה כפולה" | Everyone respects double parking. Everyone has done it. |
| **Kid card** | Hold a child | Your own preferred. Not strictly required. |

## Defending against others using this skill

When someone tries any technique on you, say "גם אני רק שאלה" and step forward half a step.
Now it's a standoff. The first to blink goes to the back.

## Common mistakes

- **Making a straight line.** Straight lines have a clear order. Stand slightly to the side, in the blob, where order is open to interpretation.
- **Looking at your phone.** The moment you look down, two people get in front of you.
- **Apologizing.** "סליחה" means you know you did something. Say "רגע רגע רגע" instead.

## Red flags

- There's a security guard managing the queue. He has seen every technique. He invented some of them.
- It's the queue for the airport bathroom. Some lines are sacred.
