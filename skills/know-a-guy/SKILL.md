# Know A Guy (יש לי מישהו)

## Overview

Every Israeli institution has two entrances: the official one, with forms and a queue,
and the real one, which is a phone call from someone's cousin.

This skill makes sure you always try the real entrance first.
It's essentially a skill that calls other people's skills.

## When to use

- Booking any medical appointment more than 3 days away
- Anything at משרד הפנים, the municipality, or the tax authority
- Hiring a plumber, electrician, lawyer, accountant, or moving company
- A kid needs to get into a specific class, gan, or school
- Any form that says "יש להמתין עד 21 ימי עבודה"

## Workflow

1. **Do not start the official process.** Starting it gets you a reference number, and a reference number gets you in line.
2. **Broadcast the need.** Post in your family WhatsApp group: "מישהו מכיר מישהו ב...?"
3. **Wait for a match.** Someone always knows someone. It might take 2 hours. It will not take 21 working days.
4. **Get introduced.** The connection sends a message: "תגיד שאתה מטעם שלי".
5. **Call the contact.** Open with the name of who sent you, before your own name.
6. **Owe a favor.** Note it down. It will be collected at the least convenient time, usually a wedding invitation to someone you've never met.

## Lookup order

Search your network in this order, and stop at the first hit:

1. Immediate family
2. Someone from your army unit
3. A cousin's spouse
4. The parent of a kid in your kid's class
5. Your mother's neighbor (your mother will find this person even if you don't ask her to)
6. "Some guy" your friend used once and was "ממש בסדר"
7. The official channel (last resort, and you should feel a little ashamed)

## Phrases

- "אני מטעם של..." ("I'm coming on behalf of..."): opens doors
- "הוא אמר שתעזור לי" ("he said you'd help me"): even if he didn't say that
- "אני לא רוצה לעקוף אף אחד, רק..." ("I don't want to cut in front of anyone, I just..."): said right before cutting in front of everyone

## Common mistakes

- **Mentioning the connection too late.** Lead with the name. After you've explained your problem, you're just another caller.
- **Using a weak connection on a big ask.** Save the army friend for the big things. Use your mom's neighbor on the small ones.
- **Asking the connection directly for the thing.** You don't ask for the thing. You ask if they "can check something". The thing happens by itself.

## Red flags

- The contact says "אין בעיה, תשלח לי מייל". That's the official channel in disguise. Call back in a week and ask if they "saw the email".
- Your connection is in a public role where helping you would be a real conflict of interest. That's when "know a guy" stops being a cultural quirk and starts being a news story. Use the queue.
