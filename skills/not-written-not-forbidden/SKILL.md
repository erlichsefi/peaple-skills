# Not Written, Not Forbidden (לא כתוב שאסור)

## Overview

A rule only forbids what it literally says. Everything else is permitted.
And because no rule can list everything, almost everything is permitted.

This skill teaches you to read any rule, sign or regulation, find the space
between its words, and live in that space comfortably and with a clear conscience.

**Core principle:** if it's not written that it's forbidden, it's allowed.
**Corollary:** if it *is* written that it's forbidden, check exactly what was written.

## When to use

- You want to do something, and nobody has said no yet
- There's a sign, but it's vague, old, faded, or in only one language
- The rule names a specific thing, and you're doing a *slightly different* thing
- Someone says "זה ברור שאסור" ("it's obvious that it's forbidden"). Obvious is not written.

Pairs well with [ask-to-be-excused](../ask-to-be-excused/SKILL.md): first look for a gray area, and only if there truly is none, DM for an exemption.

## Workflow

1. **Find the exact wording.** Not what the rule *means*. What it *says*.
2. **Do the literal reading.** Look for the gap between the words and the intent. There always is one.
3. **Apply an interpretation technique** (see below) until you reach "allowed".
4. **Say the justification out loud once, to yourself.** If it sounds reasonable in your own voice, it's legal.
5. **Do it confidently.** Hesitating makes it look like you know it's forbidden.
6. **Prepare the sentence for when someone catches you:** "איפה כתוב שאסור?" ("where does it say it's forbidden?")

## Interpretation techniques

| Technique | Rule says | You conclude |
|---|---|---|
| **Narrow definition** | "No parking" | Stopping isn't parking. Waiting with the engine running is stopping. |
| **Time loophole** | "No swimming after 18:00" | I got in at 17:58. I'm *continuing* to swim. |
| **Quantity loophole** | "No dogs" | It's one dog. A small one. Practically a cat. |
| **Spirit of the rule** | "No outside food" | The rule is about cleanliness. My sandwich is clean. |
| **Who it's for** | "Residents only" | I'm almost a resident. I've been here a lot. |
| **Missing sign** | (no sign) | No sign means no rule. |
| **Sign facing the other way** | "No entry" (on the far side) | I didn't see it from my side. |
| **Rule from before** | Any rule older than a year | That was during the previous ועד. |
| **Technically** | Anything | The word "technically" before a sentence makes it true. |

## Self-talk script

When a little voice says "this probably isn't okay", answer it in order:

1. "Everyone does it."
2. "It's not like I'm hurting anyone."
3. "If they wanted it forbidden, they'd have written it."
4. "And even if it's written, they didn't mean it for this case."
5. "I'll be quick."

If the voice is still talking after step 5, it's too sensitive and can be ignored.

## Defending yourself when challenged

Say these lines in order and stop as soon as the other person sighs:

1. "איפה כתוב?" ("where does it say?")
2. "זה לא בדיוק אותו דבר" ("that's not exactly the same thing")
3. "אז למה הוא עושה את זה?" (point at someone else)
4. "לא ידעתי" ("I didn't know"). Allowed even after steps 1-3 showed you knew.
5. "בסדר בסדר, רגוע" ("okay, okay, relax"). Then keep doing it, just slower.

## Common mistakes

- **Asking before you do it.** Asking creates the possibility of a "no". Without a "no", you're in the gray area. With a "no", you're in the black area.
- **Reading the rule in full.** The second paragraph usually closes the loophole from the first paragraph. Stop after the first one.
- **Admitting you understood the intent.** Intent can't be proven. Keep it that way.
- **Applying this skill to rules you made.** Your rules are written clearly and leave no room for interpretation, and everyone should respect that.

## Red flags (stop and reconsider)

- The rule has a fine amount printed on it. There is no gray area that beats a printed amount.
- There's a camera. Cameras don't do interpretation.
- The person enforcing it is a lifeguard. A lifeguard with a megaphone is a higher authority than any wording.
