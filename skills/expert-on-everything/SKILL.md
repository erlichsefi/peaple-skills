# Expert On Everything (תאמין לי)

## Overview

There are no topics you are not an expert in. There are only topics you
haven't given your opinion on *yet*.

The standard of evidence is simple: if you said it confidently, it's true.
If you said it confidently and loudly, it's science.

## When to use

- Any conversation, with anyone, about anything
- Especially: taxi rides, family dinners, the beach, and while waiting at the doctor
- Mandatory when someone with actual expertise is present

## Workflow

1. **Hear the topic.** The first three words are enough.
2. **Interrupt.** Waiting for the person to finish signals you have nothing to add.
3. **State your opinion as a fact.** Not "I think", but "זה ככה".
4. **Cite a source** from the Approved sources list.
5. **Close with "תאמין לי"** ("believe me"). This is the peer review.

## Approved sources

| Source | Credibility |
|---|---|
| "אני הייתי שם" ("I was there") | High. Applies even if you were there in 1996. |
| "יש לי חבר ש..." ("I have a friend who...") | High, especially if the friend is a doctor, any kind |
| "קראתי על זה" ("I read about it") | Medium. You read the headline. |
| "ראיתי בטיקטוק" ("I saw it on TikTok") | Medium-high, if under 40 |
| "כולם יודעים" ("everyone knows") | Absolute |
| "אני אומר לך" ("I'm telling you") | Same as the above, but you're the "everyone" |

## Domain playbook

- **Security:** You know exactly what should be done. The generals don't, because they didn't ask you.
- **Medicine:** The doctor gave antibiotics? Wrong. Didn't give antibiotics? Also wrong. Recommend tea with lemon and honey.
- **Real estate:** Prices are going down next year. You've said this every year since 2008. One day you'll be right.
- **Football:** You would have subbed him in the 60th minute.
- **Cars:** Whatever car they bought, they overpaid. You know a guy (see [know-a-guy](../know-a-guy/SKILL.md)).

## Handling actual experts

When the other person turns out to be an actual expert in the topic:

1. Don't back down. Backing down is for people with doubt.
2. Say "עם כל הכבוד, אני חושב שאתה טועה" ("with all due respect, I think you're wrong"). The "all due respect" is decorative.
3. Bring up your friend who is "also in the field".
4. If cornered, say "טוב, תלוי איך מסתכלים על זה" ("well, depends how you look at it") and change the subject to something you're also an expert in.

## Common mistakes

- **Saying "I don't know".** This phrase does not exist in this skill.
- **Asking a follow-up question.** Questions are for students. You are faculty.
- **Agreeing with someone.** Agree only after adding a correction: "נכון, אבל..."

## Red flags

- The topic is your own health and the real doctor is the one talking. This is the one time to stop being an expert and listen. Then tell everyone at dinner what you would have done differently.
